## PART 4: CRITICAL DISCREPANCIES FOUND

### 1. Margaret Dougal Telfer — MISSING from SVG
- In people.json, **Margaret Dougal Telfer (1849–1936)** is listed as:
  - Spouse of **Adam Francis Telfer** (not in tree)
  - **Daughter of Francis Telfer (1809–1895)** and Margaret Wright
  - Half-sister of John Telfer (1847–1929)
- She's Francis's daughter from an earlier marriage, meaning the SVG shows Margaret Wright as Francis's wife but doesn't show any of the children from his first two marriages!

### 2. Mitchell Telfer's Biological Mother
- In JSON: Mitchell's parents are **Crystal Rose McDonald** and **Mark Telfer**
- In SVG: Mitchell appears under Mark & Kylie as their child
- Neither Crystal Rose McDonald nor the discrepancy is noted in the SVG

### 3. David Telfer's Father
- In JSON: David Telfer's parents are **Penny Telfer** and **"Unknown"**
- In SVG: David appears as Timothy Neil's son (alongside Mark and Amy)
- The JSON doesn't list Timothy Neil as David's father

### 4. Jono Telfer — No JSON Record
- Jono appears in the SVG as Grantley Keith's son but has no separate JSON record (unlike Kristin Stefanoff and Nick Telfer who both have records)

### 5. The "Other" John Telfer (1840–1913)
- There are TWO John Telfers in the family:
  - **John Telfer (1847–1929)** — IS in the SVG (son of Francis & Margaret Wright, main line)
  - **John Telfer (1840–1913)** — NOT in SVG (son of James Telfer (1796–1863) & Elizabeth Beattie, married Martha, had 14 children)
- They are different people, essentially first cousins
