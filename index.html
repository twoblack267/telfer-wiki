Telfer Wiki

A digital archive for the descendants of John Telfer of Castleton — from the Scottish lowlands to the South Australian plains

This is a living family history. Nine generations of Telfers — shepherds and farmers, miners and merchants — traced from the hills of Roxburghshire, Scotland (~1715) through the copper boom of South Australia, across the Eyre Peninsula, and into the present day.

Every person here is blood of the same root. Every page tells a piece of the story. This is our family's record — built for the living, remembering the gone.
