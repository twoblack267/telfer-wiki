    Colin Roy was born at Tantanoola in 1889, the eighth child of John Telfer (1840–1913) and Martha Henstridge. He died at Surry Hills, New South Wales in 1964, suggesting he migrated interstate like several of his siblings. No marriage record has been found, and he may have been a lifelong bachelor. The book records his name in the family tree listings of John Telfer's children. (From the Borders to the Bush, p.258)
