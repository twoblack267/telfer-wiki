Family Stories

Ernie was the first white baby born at Willow Plains, the family's pioneering property near Hawker. The book relates that he was born in 1877 in a galvanised iron room and tent — the only shelter the family had at the time. His uncle, Walter "Watt" Telfer, once described young Ernie as "a larrikin," suggesting he had a spirited nature. As an adult, Ernest became a missionary with the United Aborigines Mission and wrote a book titled Amongst Australian Aborigines, which became a valuable source for the family history. He also served as the family's photographer for many key events. He married Florence Nicholas and raised a family including John Ernest, Jessica Veronica, Lloyd Wilbur and Gweneth Miranda. (From the Borders to the Bush, pp.127, 141–142, 178)
