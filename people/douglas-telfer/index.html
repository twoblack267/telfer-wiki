Life Summary

Douglas Telfer was the seventh child of John Telfer and Martha Clara Henstridge. Born at Mount Gambier, South Australia on 7 October 1887, he married Lilian Daisy Dawson in 1910. He died in 1956 at Brunswick East, Victoria, suggesting he relocated across the border at some point in his life — possibly for work. His death location in Victoria aligns with several of his siblings who also settled outside South Australia (Colin Roy in NSW, Alan Dale in NSW, Alma Tressy in Victoria).

⚠️ Death year uncertainty: The 1956 date is generally accepted in primary family trees. Some records suggest a Douglas Telfer whose ashes were interred at St John's Cemetery, Gordon (New South Wales) on 29 November 1967, while other records point to a Reverend Douglas Telfer operating in Canada. Further verification via NSW BDM registry is needed to confirm whether the 1967 Gordon ashes belong to the Tantanoola Douglas.

Family Stories

Douglas was born at Mount Gambier in 1887, the seventh child of John Telfer (1840–1913) and Martha Henstridge. He appears in a 1904 wedding party photograph at Koppio Station — a young man seated beside his father James Telfer at the wedding of his sister Agnes Lily to Edwin James Barraud. He later married Lilian Daisy Dawson. (From the Borders to the Bush, pp.203, 258)
