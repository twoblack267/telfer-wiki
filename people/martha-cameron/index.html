Life Summary

Martha Telfer was born on 18 April 1873 at Black Heath (also recorded as Blackheath), Tungkillo, South Australia, the fourth child and third daughter of John Telfer (1840–1913) by his first wife Martha Allen (1840–1876). The family lived at Honeysuckle Flat and Black Heath during her early years before moving to the Tantanoola district in the South East.

On 29 March 1899 at Tantanoola, SA, Martha married Malcolm Duncan Cameron (1873–1935), a farmer. They had at least one known child: a son, Malcolm Murray Cameron, born 2 August 1905 at Mount Gambier SA.

Martha died on 4 August 1964 at Mount Gambier, South Australia, aged 91, and was predeceased by her husband by 29 years. Her burial location has not been positively identified — no FindAGrave record has been found, and her 1964 death was too late for Trove newspaper death notices.

Family Stories

Martha — known as Mattie — was the fourth and youngest child of John Telfer and his first wife Martha Allen, born on 18 April 1873 at Black Heath. She married Malcolm Duncan Cameron on 29 March 1899 at Tantanoola. Malcolm was a man of public affairs: during his time in Parliament he served on the Works Committee, eventually becoming its Chairman, and was a lifelong member of the Liberal Party. He was also an Elder of the Presbyterian Church for thirty years.

Mattie and Malcolm raised five children — Murray, Ivan, Lorna, Mavis and Jack — in the South East district. Malcolm died on 1 March 1935, and Mattie lived on for another twenty-nine years, passing away at Mount Gambier in 1964 at the age of 91.

(From the Borders to the Bush, pp.238–239)
