Life Summary

Dinah Flavel was born on 10 June 1821 at Pytchley, Northamptonshire, England — the daughter of Joseph Flavel (1797–1840) and Mary Bayes. Little is known of her childhood in England.

She married Francis Telfer on 4 May 1839 — just four months after the death of his first wife Jean Murray. The marriage took place in Adelaide, South Australia. Dinah was 18 years old; Francis was 30 with three young children from his first marriage.

Dinah gave birth to a daughter, Jean, on 7 August 1840 at Chain of Ponds in the Adelaide Hills. She died just over three years later, on 20 March 1844 at Chain of Ponds, barely 22 years old. She was buried at West Terrace Cemetery, Adelaide.

Her brief life was marked by the frontier conditions of early South Australia — a young English immigrant who married a widowed Scottish farmer with young children, had a daughter, and died before reaching age 23.

Family Stories

The Flavel Family Voyage

According to Donald Telfer's family history (1986), Chapter 2, pp. 23–24: Dinah travelled to South Australia aboard the Prince George with her parents Joseph and Mary Flavel and her six siblings, alongside the Telfer family. Her brother William Flavel later described walking barefoot from Port Misery through the sandhills, the heat running so painfully between his toes that he "had to run from one bush to another till I got clear of the sand." Dinah married Francis Telfer barely four months after the death of his first wife Jean Murray, becoming stepmother to three young children — James (7), Esther (5), and Elizabeth (4).
