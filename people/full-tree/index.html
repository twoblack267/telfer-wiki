The Telfer Line — Family Tree

This is the Telfer Family Wiki — a digital archive for the descendants of John Telfer of Castleton (~1715, Scottish Lowlands). What started in the hills of Roxburghshire became a story that crossed oceans: from Scotland to the copper mines of South Australia, across the Eyre Peninsula, and down to the present day.

This tree follows the Telfer name — every known Telfer by birth and the people they married, from John Telfer of Castleton (~1715) through to the latest generation. We track each couple and their children, but once a daughter marries and her children take a different surname, that branch ends here. It's the Telfer line — not every family that married into it.
