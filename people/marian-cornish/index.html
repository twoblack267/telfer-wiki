Life Summary

Marian Telfer was born around 1896 in Tantanoola, South Australia, the youngest child of John Telfer (1840–1913) and his second wife Martha Clara Henstridge. She was the 16th child of John Telfer — and the youngest from his second marriage to Martha Clara Henstridge.

She married Kenneth Cornish on 21 June 1924 at Gumeracha, South Australia. The wedding was announced in The Chronicle (Adelaide) on 28 June 1924, describing her as "Marian, youngest daughter of the late Mr. and Mrs. J. Telfer, Tantanoola" — confirming both her parents were deceased by that date (John died 1913, Martha Clara died 1921).

She died in 1977.
