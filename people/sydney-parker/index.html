Life Summary

Sydney Hugh Parker was the third child and third son of William Henry Parker (1893–1968) and Caroline Edna Lawrie (1890–1957), born at the family property at Long Plains, South Australia. He married Lilian Jean Kelly on 7 Jul 1945 in Riverton, South Australia.

He died at Boatswain Point, a small coastal locality near Robe in South Australia's Limestone Coast region, on 7 Mar 1964 at age 41. He is buried alongside his wife at Robe Cemetery.

The couple had no children.
