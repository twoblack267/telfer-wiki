Life Summary

Margaret Wright was the third wife of Francis Telfer (1809–1895). She married him in South Australia after the deaths of his first wife Jean Murray (d. 1839) and second wife Dinah Flavel.

She was the mother of:

  • John Telfer (1847–1929) — Mark's great-great-grandfather
  • George Wright Telfer — who later farmed at Willaston
  • Margaret Dougal Telfer
Margaret Wright died on 13 September 1892 at Kersbrook and is buried at Kersbrook Cemetery (General, Section B, Row 1, Plot B13). Her death came about three years before Francis, which prompted his eventual move from the farm at Kersbrook to live with his son at Gawler.

About the Three Wives of Francis Telfer

Francis Telfer married three times — an unusual number that sometimes causes confusion in genealogical records:

  • Jean Murray — married in Scotland, mother of James (1832), Esther Jane (1834), Elizabeth (1836). Jean died 22 Jan 1839, shortly after arrival in SA.
  • Dinah Flavel — second wife in South Australia
  • Margaret Wright — third wife and mother of John and the younger children

    • Some trees simply list "Margaret Telfer" as Francis's wife without specifying which marriage, leading to contradictions between sources.

    Family Stories

    The Stirling Castle Legend

    According to Donald Telfer's family history (1986), Chapter 2, p. 27: Family tradition held that Margaret Wright was "born in Stirling Castle." Which room — the main bedchamber, a guest room, or the servants' quarters — was left for speculation. The last reigning monarch to live at Stirling Castle was James VI (1567–1625), so the story may refer to her family's residence within the castle precincts rather than the royal apartments.
