Life Summary

Ruth Telfer was the youngest of the ten Telfer children born to Francis and Amy Telfer. She was born at Saddleworth, South Australia, on 26 October 1931 — her birth name was Pamela Ruth Telfer, but the family called her Ruth.

Her sister Jean McMurtrie wrote in her memoir:

\"Our youngest sister Ruth was born while we were at Saddleworth, mother was 48, and that was very hard for her, but Ruth being so much younger than the rest of us has been a real blessing to us all.\"

Ruth appears in a cherished family photograph circa 1936 at approximately age 6, riding the family horse Duchess with her brother Murray (then aged ~12), accompanied by the family dog.

The photograph captures a tender moment of rural childhood on the Eyre Peninsula — Ruth beaming with joy in her bob haircut and light summer top, bare legs dangling, while Murray holds the reins responsibly. Duchess, a bay with distinctive white blaze and four white socks, was clearly a beloved family horse.

Further details of Ruth's life — marriage, children, later residence — are currently being researched.
