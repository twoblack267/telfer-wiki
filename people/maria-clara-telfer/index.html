Life Summary

Maria Clara Telfer was the second child of John Telfer and his second wife Martha Clara Henstridge, born around 1878. She was the elder sister of ten siblings from this marriage and was named with the middle name Clara after her mother. She died young, on 22 December 1903 at Tantanoola, South Australia, at approximately 25 years of age, never having married. She predeceased both her mother (who died in 1921) and father (who died in 1913) by ten and eight years respectively — an unusually early passing that cut short a life that might have continued long enough to see the family's later expansion.

Family Stories

Maria Clara Telfer was the seventh child of John Telfer and the second child of his marriage to Martha Clara Henstridge. She died young at just 25 years of age, never having married. Her memorial card — printed in black with gold lettering — bore her photograph circled with the poignant words "Though lost to sight, To memory dear." Below the photograph was inscribed: "In Loving Memory of Maria Clara Telfer." Her early passing in December 1903 at Tantanoola was a sadness visited upon the family well before her mother's death in 1921 and her father's in 1913.

(From the Borders to the Bush, pp.242, 248–249)
