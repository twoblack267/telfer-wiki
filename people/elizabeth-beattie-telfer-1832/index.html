Life Summary

Elizabeth Beattie Telfer, known as "Betty" in the family, was the second child and eldest daughter of James and Elizabeth Telfer. She was the first of the four siblings to leave Scotland, emigrating to South Australia before her brothers James and Robert. She married John Charles Dillon in 1864.

Family Stories

Elizabeth Beattie Telfer — known in the family as Betty — was born in 1832 at Castleton in Scotland. She was the only daughter among the five children of James and Elizabeth, and she was the first of them to leave Scotland. She came to South Australia at some point before 1854, settling with their Uncle Francis Telfer at "Macoota" in the Adelaide Hills. She was already here when her brothers James and Robert arrived on the "Oliver Lang" in 1854.

Betty married John Charles Dillon in 1864 at the Mount Gambier area. John had come from Ireland. They made their home in the South East of South Australia and had a large family of ten children — five sons and five daughters — though tragically, one son was stillborn and twin girls lived only 15 minutes.

The Dillon family spread through the South East region. The connection between the Telfers and Dillons runs deep — Betty was the bridge between the two families, and her descendants are still found in the Mount Gambier district today.

Betty died in 1896, aged 64. John Charles Dillon died in 1906 at the age of 79.

(From the Borders to the Bush, pp. 214–215)
