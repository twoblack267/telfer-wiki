Life Summary

James Telfer Dillon was born in 1861 at Kooringa, South Australia, the eldest son of Elizabeth Beattie Telfer and John Charles Dillon. He was named after his maternal grandfather James Telfer (1796–1863), following the Scottish naming tradition of passing the surname as a middle name.

He spent his early years in the Burra district (Kooringa) before the family moved to Lobethal and other areas. James lived to the age of 89, passing away on 16 March 1951 at Lobethal, South Australia.
