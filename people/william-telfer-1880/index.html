William Telfer

1880 – 1880
WT

Biography

Born 16 September 1880 at Willow Plains, South Australia. Son of John Telfer (1847–1929) and Caroline Amelia Masters (1842–1929). Died November 1880 aged 2 months. Buried by the side of the road at Willow Plains with a stone marker — a testament to the family's early hardships on the land.

Source: From the Borders to the Bush — body text
